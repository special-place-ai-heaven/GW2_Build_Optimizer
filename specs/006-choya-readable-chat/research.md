# Research: Choya readable chat

Checked against the tree on 2026-09-08 (branch `006-choya-readable-chat` off the Sprint 2 tip) with SymForge. Every decision names the code it lands on.

## R1. Why replies are cut, and the reply shape

**Facts.** `chat_bar::add_plated_response` (chat_bar.rs:709) keeps 600 characters of a reply and appends `...`; the rest is discarded before it reaches history, so scrolling cannot recover it. `fold_punctuation` (:686) turns `…` into `...`, which is why the cut reads as three dots. The bubble is drawn by `wrap_text` (:124, word-wrap by measuring) and `draw_bubble_text` (:210, one `add_text` per line in `pal().cream`). `theme::prose` (theme.rs:866) already renders headings, bullets and numbered lists for the About and News panes but through `text_wrapped`, not the bubble's draw list, and its own comment says "overlay fonts have no bold". Fonts: one UI face chosen by `resolve_font_id` (fonts.rs:394), plus CJK and ticker faces; no bold or italic face is loaded, though `system_fonts` lists `arialbd` and folds it away (:558).

**Decision.** Remove the cap: history stores the whole reply, `CHAT_HISTORY_CAP` (message count) stays the only bound. Replace the bubble's line layout with a span layout: a parser turns the reply into blocks (paragraph, bullet, numbered, rotation) of styled spans (plain, bold, italic, name, warn, link), a wrapper measures spans to the bubble width, and the draw pass emits one `add_text` per span with colour, plus a line under links. Bold is faux bold (the span drawn twice, one pixel apart) so it works with any chosen font; italic uses the family's italic file when `resolve_font_id`'s family has one on disk (`segoeuii.ttf`, `ariali.ttf`, `georgiai.ttf`), otherwise the muted colour. Names the game data knows are found by longest match against the `GameDb` name index the chips already use and drawn in the accent colour; the model may also mark emphasis with `**` and `_`. A line starting with `!` or `⚠` is drawn in `WARN`. `A -> B -> C` and `A → B → C` are rotation lines and render with `→` in the accent colour. URLs are underlined and open through the existing `render_source_link` path.

**Prompt.** `prompts::chat_refinement_prompt_with_tools` (prompts.rs:290) asks for "explanation: 2-4 sentences" three times and for `explanation` inside JSON. One appended paragraph, REPLY SHAPE, replaces the sentence count: at most eight short lines, `- ` bullets, `**bold**` on names, `->` for rotations, `!` for a warning line, no paragraph over two sentences, no filler; and the same paragraph goes into `new_build_prompt_with_tools` and `improve_build_prompt_with_tools` since they share the explanation field. Newlines in the JSON string are `\n`, which the parser already accepts. The boundary is released by the clarification; only that paragraph and the three "2-4 sentences" phrases change.

**Alternatives.** Loading a bold face per family: correct but a font-picker matrix (12 languages, custom files); faux bold covers every face and costs one extra draw call per bold span. Rendering markdown through `theme::prose`: it cannot draw inside the bubble's draw-list rectangle and has no spans.

## R2. Build tabs

**Facts.** `render_comparison` (comparison.rs:519) draws a `Selectable` per suggestion only when there is more than one, identical in colour, and toggles current vs optimized elsewhere through `show_optimized` and `ChatSource`. `BuildSuggestion.source_url` is non-empty only for published builds; `site_colour` (:445) already maps GuildJen, Hardstuck and Snowcrows to fixed colours; `theme::CURRENT` and `theme::OPTIMIZED` are the semantic colours the diff columns use. `adopt_provider_pick` pushes beside existing tabs (or replaces the same URL); `saveload::apply_loaded_suggestion` (:426) replaces the whole strip.

**Decision.** A `TabKind` derived, not stored: `Current` (the pseudo-tab for the equipped build, shown whenever a character is selected), `Optimized` (a suggestion with empty `source_url`), `Published(site)` (non-empty). Tab fill is `lerp(pal().chip_idle_fill, kind colour, 0.30)`, rim the kind colour at the chip rim alpha, selected fill the kind colour at 0.85 with `gold_button_text`; kind colour is `CURRENT`, `OPTIMIZED` or `site_colour`. The strip always renders when a character is selected or more than one suggestion exists; selecting Current sets `show_optimized = false`. Published labels are `"{site} · {build}"`. `apply_loaded_suggestion` pushes (replacing a tab with the same label) instead of clearing. A theme test asserts a minimum RGB distance between the three fills and the panel background for every preset and the custom-theme extremes, the same way the WCAG gate tests do.

## R3. Published cards

**Facts.** `refresh_provider_picks` (provider_picks.rs:21) keys the cards on the *selected* suggestion's profession, mode, role and specs and clears them when the key changes; `render_chat_bar` (chat_bar.rs:222) draws the cards only under the newest reply, and only when that reply has `open_result` or `build_failed`. Today's disappearance was both: the scoring fallback pushed a new Ritualist suggestion, the key changed, the Reaper filter emptied the list, and the newest reply had no card anyway.

**Decision.** Cards belong to the plate: the key is built from the newest suggestion with empty `source_url` (Choya's or the optimizer's plate), not from whatever is selected, and the cards render under the newest reply whose `open_result` is true, not only the last message. A follow-up reply without a plate leaves both untouched. A new plate replaces them; `Clear` empties them with the history.

## R4. Fallback by request kind

**Facts.** The worker keeps `fallback_reference` (chat_flow.rs:237), built by `reference_build` (:1125) for the *character's* profession, and serves it with `choya.fallback_reference` on any model failure. `wants_a_build` (:1411), `asks_about_own_build` (:1436) and `wished_elite_spec` (:1497) already read the message. `plate_shortfall` runs the referee on a plate, and `gemini_tools::score_full_build` returns the verdict JSON for a whole build.

**Decision.** A pure `RequestKind::classify(message, has_plate) -> Build { elite: Option<String> } | AboutPlate | Chat` in chat_flow: `AboutPlate` when a plate exists and the message contains score / gate / simulate / why / explain / rate / compare / what was not; `Build` from `wants_a_build` with the wished elite; `Chat` otherwise. The fallback branches on it: `Build` runs `reference_build` for the profession that owns the wished elite (looked up in `db.specializations`), with the elite locked; `AboutPlate` formats `score_full_build`'s verdict (viable, gates, score, coverage note) as bullets and pushes no suggestion; `Chat` replies with the plain line and two next steps. The `ChatMessage` gains `build_failed` only for `Build`, so published cards appear only where they help.

## R5. Thinking bubble, stall, Stop, Retry

**Facts.** All three providers stream: `sse.rs` for OpenAI-compatible and OpenRouter (content, tool-call and `reasoning_details` deltas, in order, with a cancellation predicate polled between lines), `anthropic::read_anthropic_stream` (event SSE), Gemini `streamGenerateContent?alt=sse` (gemini.rs:155). `spawn_worker` (state.rs:392) already binds the worker's `CancellationToken` to the transports through a thread-local predicate because `LlmClient` is `&self`; `state.cancel_token` is one token for all workers, renewed by cancel-and-replace (state.rs:465). `chat_wait_started` and `optimize_stage` drive the "Choya is thinking… 04:37" bubble (kitchen.rs:61); `optimize_stage` is set once per request. `on_progress` reports per round only. Reasoning is requested from OpenRouter through `reasoning.effort` (openai_compat.rs:244); Gemini and Anthropic thinking are not requested today. `round_secs` is collected but only the handshake is logged.

**Decision.** A thread-local `llm::live` sink, installed by `spawn_worker` next to the cancellation predicate, that the three stream readers feed: `reasoning(delta)`, `content(delta)`, `tool_call(name)`, `step(name)`. The sink writes into `Arc<Mutex<LiveOutput>>` held in `state.main.chat_live`: current step, step start, last activity instant, reasoning text, content text, tool activity lines. The bubble reads it every frame: collapsed shows the step and elapsed seconds; expanded shows the newest of reasoning, else content, else tool activity, and the stall line "nothing for N s" when `last_activity` is older than 20 s. Reasoning exposure per provider, recorded here: OpenRouter and OpenAI-compatible models send `reasoning_details` when the model reasons (already parsed); Gemini gets `thinking_config.include_thoughts: true` and its `thought: true` parts go to `reasoning`; Anthropic `thinking_delta` events go to `reasoning` when extended thinking is on; otherwise the bubble shows the content stream; a provider that neither streams nor reasons shows tool activity and the step timer with the note that the text arrives at once. No time limit ends a request; the 150 s `CLOSING_REQUEST_TIMEOUT` becomes the read-idle timeout of the transport only (a socket that sends nothing for 150 s is dead), which the bubble reports as a stall long before.

**Stop.** A Stop button on the thinking bubble calls the state's cancel-and-replace, bumps `chat_epoch`, sets `waiting = false`, and pushes the partial content (if any) as the reply with a "stopped" marker. The transport notices between SSE lines; a read blocked on a silent socket ends at the idle timeout, invisible to the player because the epoch already retired it.

**Retry.** A Retry button on a stopped or failed reply re-sends the same user message with a `continuation` note in the kitchen brief: "You were answering this and stopped after: <partial>. Continue from there; do not repeat it." Without partial output it re-sends plainly and the bubble says "starting over".

**Log.** One `nexus::log` line per step from the worker: `Choya <step>: <outcome> in <secs>s` for handshake, reference, each round (tool names), closing, fallback, stop.

## R7. Testing strategy

Pure functions get unit tests: the markup parser and wrapper (blocks, spans, bold/italic/link detection, rotation arrows, no cut at any length), `TabKind` derivation and the tint-contrast gate over presets, the provider-picks key from the plate, `RequestKind::classify` and the fallback selection, `LiveOutput` stall computation, the log line format, the continuation brief. Rendering and streaming are checked in-game through quickstart §1–§6 with the DLL; the `choya_live` example (owned, untouched) is the acceptance harness for the model side. No scoring constant, gate or simulator rule changes; `pve_output_unchanged_by_conditional_tagging` and the Sprint 2 experiments stay green.
